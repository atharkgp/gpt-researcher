# Financial Report on NVIDIA Corporation

## Introduction

NVIDIA Corporation, a leading player in the semiconductor industry, has consistently demonstrated its prowess in developing cutting-edge technologies, particularly in graphics processing units (GPUs) and artificial intelligence (AI) platforms. This report provides a comprehensive analysis of NVIDIA's financial performance, highlighting key financial metrics, revenue streams, and strategic initiatives that have contributed to its robust growth trajectory. The report draws on recent financial data and insights to offer an in-depth understanding of NVIDIA's financial health and future prospects.

## Financial Performance Overview

### Revenue and Growth

NVIDIA's financial performance in recent years has been nothing short of remarkable. For the fiscal year ending January 28, 2024, NVIDIA reported an annual revenue of $60.92 billion, marking a staggering 125.85% growth from the previous fiscal year ([NVIDIA Corp 10-K Annual Report February 2024](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/nvda/0001045810-24-000029.htm)). This growth was driven by strong demand across its product segments, particularly in data centers and gaming.

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, NVIDIA achieved a record quarterly revenue of $22.1 billion, representing a 22% increase from the third quarter and a 265% surge from the same period last year ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-corp-nvda-shatters-records-215227111.html)). The data center segment was a significant contributor, with revenue reaching $18.4 billion for the quarter, up 27% from Q3 and a staggering 409% year-over-year growth.

### Gross Margin and Profitability

NVIDIA's gross margin has also shown significant improvement, reaching 76.0% in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, up from 74.0% in the third quarter and 63.3% from the previous year ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-corp-nvda-shatters-records-215227111.html)). This expansion in gross margin underscores NVIDIA's ability to increase sales profitably, a key indicator of financial health.

The company's net income for Q4 jumped to $12.3 billion, a 33% increase from Q3 and a 769% increase year-over-year. Earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter were $4.93, up 33% from the previous quarter and up 765% from the previous year ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-corp-nvda-shatters-records-215227111.html)).

### Operating Expenses

NVIDIA's operating expenses for fiscal 2024 were approximately $11.33 billion, which included research and development expenses of $8.675 billion and sales, general, and administrative expenses of $2.654 billion ([NVIDIA Corp 10-K Annual Report February 2024](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/nvda/0001045810-24-000029.htm)). These expenses reflect NVIDIA's ongoing investment in innovation and market expansion.

### Cash Flow and Financial Position

NVIDIA's financial position remains strong, with a net cash flow from operating activities of $16.591 billion for the nine months ended October 29, 2023 ([NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Third Quarter Fiscal 2024](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2024)). The company's robust cash flow generation supports its strategic investments and shareholder returns.

## Segment Analysis

### Compute & Networking

The Compute & Networking segment, which includes NVIDIA's data center accelerated computing platform, networking, and AI solutions, has been a major growth driver. For the fiscal year ended January 28, 2024, this segment generated $47.405 billion in revenue, a significant increase from $15.068 billion in the previous year ([NVIDIA Corp 10-K Annual Report February 2024](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/nvda/0001045810-24-000029.htm)).

### Graphics

The Graphics segment, encompassing gaming GPUs, professional visualization, and automotive platforms, reported revenue of $13.517 billion for fiscal 2024. While this represents growth from the previous year, the segment's contribution to overall revenue was overshadowed by the explosive growth in the Compute & Networking segment ([NVIDIA Corp 10-K Annual Report February 2024](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/nvda/0001045810-24-000029.htm)).

## Strategic Initiatives and Future Outlook

### AI and Accelerated Computing

NVIDIA's strategic focus on AI and accelerated computing has positioned it at the forefront of the industry. The company's GPUs and AI platforms are integral to various sectors, including gaming, professional visualization, and automotive. NVIDIA's data center growth has been fueled by strong demand for generative AI training and inference on its Hopper platform ([NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for First Quarter Fiscal 2025](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-first-quarter-fiscal-2025)).

### Product Innovation

NVIDIA continues to innovate with new product offerings and technologies. The company announced the NVIDIA HGX H200 with the new NVIDIA H200 Tensor Core GPU, the first GPU with HBM3e memory ([NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Third Quarter Fiscal 2024](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2024)). Additionally, NVIDIA's AI foundry service aims to accelerate the development of custom generative AI applications.

### Financial Outlook

Looking ahead, NVIDIA anticipates continued growth in fiscal 2025. The company's outlook for the first quarter of fiscal 2025 projects revenue of approximately $24.0 billion, with GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins expected to be around 76.3% and 77.0%, respectively ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-corp-nvda-shatters-records-215227111.html)). Operating expenses are projected to be around $3.5 billion on a GAAP basis and $2.5 billion on a non-GAAP basis.

## Conclusion

NVIDIA's financial performance in fiscal 2024 underscores its strong position in the semiconductor industry. The company's innovative AI and GPU technologies have driven significant revenue and profit growth, positioning NVIDIA for continued success in the evolving tech landscape. As NVIDIA continues to expand its product offerings and capitalize on the growing demand for accelerated computing and AI, it remains well-positioned for future growth and value creation for shareholders.
