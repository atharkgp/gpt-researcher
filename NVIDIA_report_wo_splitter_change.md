# Financial Report on NVIDIA Corporation

## Introduction

NVIDIA Corporation, a leading player in the semiconductor industry, has demonstrated remarkable financial performance over the years. This report delves into the financial health of NVIDIA, focusing on its fiscal year 2024 results, revenue growth, profitability, and future prospects. The analysis is based on data from various reliable sources, including the company's financial statements, earnings call transcripts, and market analysis reports.

## Fiscal Year 2024 Overview

Fiscal year 2024 was a landmark year for NVIDIA, marked by unprecedented growth and record-breaking revenues. The company's annual revenue soared to $60.9 billion, representing a 126% increase from the previous year ([TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/319409/nvidia-announces-q4-and-fiscal-2024-results-clocks-126-yoy-revenue-growth-gaming-just-1-6th-of-data-center-revenues)). This growth was largely driven by the Data Center segment, which accounted for a significant portion of the total revenue.

### Revenue Breakdown

NVIDIA's revenue for fiscal year 2024 was primarily driven by its two main segments: Compute & Networking and Graphics. The Compute & Networking segment generated $47.4 billion, while the Graphics segment contributed $13.5 billion ([NVIDIA Corporation Annual Review](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240222-nvidia-2024q4/)). The company's focus on AI and data center technologies has paid off, with gaming revenue constituting only a sixth of the data center revenues ([TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/319409/nvidia-announces-q4-and-fiscal-2024-results-clocks-126-yoy-revenue-growth-gaming-just-1-6th-of-data-center-revenues)).

### Quarterly Performance

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, NVIDIA reported quarterly sales of $22.1 billion, a 265% increase from the previous year ([GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240222-nvidia-2024q4/)). This strong performance was attributed to the robust demand for NVIDIA's Hopper GPU computing platform, which is widely used in AI applications, including large language models (LLMs) and recommendation engines.

## Profitability

NVIDIA's profitability metrics have shown significant improvement in fiscal year 2024. The company's gross margin increased to 72.7%, up by 15.8 percentage points from the previous year ([NVIDIA Corporation Annual Review](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240222-nvidia-2024q4/)). Operating income surged by 681% to $33.0 billion, while diluted earnings per share rose by 586% to $11.93 ([NVIDIA Corporation Annual Review](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240222-nvidia-2024q4/)).

### Earnings Per Share

The remarkable growth in earnings per share (EPS) is a testament to NVIDIA's strong operational efficiency and strategic investments in high-growth areas. The company's EPS growth rate significantly outpaced its 10-year average of 34.30%, highlighting the acceleration in its earnings trajectory ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/unveiling-the-hidden-truths-a-deep-analysis-of-nvidias-financial-performance-and-investment-potential-202402252355)).

### Free Cash Flow

NVIDIA's free cash flow experienced a dramatic increase of 269.10% in the past year, maintaining an average annual growth rate of 28.50% over the past decade ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/unveiling-the-hidden-truths-a-deep-analysis-of-nvidias-financial-performance-and-investment-potential-202402252355)). This robust cash flow dynamic underscores the company's strong financial position and its ability to fund future growth initiatives.

## Market Position and Future Prospects

NVIDIA's strategic focus on AI and data center technologies has positioned it as a leader in the semiconductor industry. The company's growth is supported by the anticipated Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) in the AI market over the coming decade ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/unveiling-the-hidden-truths-a-deep-analysis-of-nvidias-financial-performance-and-investment-potential-202402252355)).

### Stock Valuation

Despite the impressive growth, NVIDIA's stock valuation remains a topic of discussion among investors. The company's stock has soared nearly tenfold since the beginning of 2023, and its future revenue and earnings might already be priced in ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2024/06/28/nvidia-revenue-growth-last-25-years-will-blow-mind/)). However, NVIDIA's momentum is expected to continue, with Wall Street forecasts suggesting a revenue generation of $120.5 billion for fiscal 2025 ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2024/06/28/nvidia-revenue-growth-last-25-years-will-blow-mind/)).

### Risks and Challenges

NVIDIA faces several risks and challenges that could impact its future performance. These include global economic conditions, reliance on third-party manufacturers, and technological competition ([NVIDIA Corporation Annual Review](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240222-nvidia-2024q4/)). The company must navigate these challenges while continuing to innovate and expand its product offerings.

## Conclusion

NVIDIA's financial performance in fiscal year 2024 was nothing short of extraordinary. The company achieved record-breaking revenues and profitability, driven by its strategic focus on AI and data center technologies. Looking ahead, NVIDIA is well-positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for AI solutions, although it must remain vigilant in managing potential risks and challenges.
