# Financial Report of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD)

## Introduction

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a global semiconductor company that has been a significant player in the technology industry for over five decades. The company is renowned for its innovation in high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. AMD's products include x86 microprocessors (CPUs), graphics processing units (GPUs), accelerated processing units (APUs), chipsets, data center and professional GPUs, embedded processors, semi-custom System-on-Chip (SoC) products, and more. This report provides an in-depth analysis of AMD's financial performance, focusing on its recent financial results and strategic initiatives.

## Overview of AMD's Financial Performance

### Fiscal Year 2024 Financial Results

AMD has reported robust financial results for the fiscal year 2024. The company's revenue for the third quarter of 2024 was $6.8 billion, representing an 18% increase year-over-year. The gross margin for the quarter was 50%, with an operating income of $724 million and a net income of $771 million. On a non-GAAP basis, the gross margin was 54%, operating income was $1.7 billion, and net income was $1.5 billion ([AMD Reports Third Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1224/amd-reports-third-quarter-2024-financial-results)).

AMD's strong performance was driven by higher sales of EPYC and Instinct data center products and robust demand for Ryzen PC processors. The company's data center segment revenue reached a record $3.5 billion, up 122% year-over-year, primarily due to the strong ramp of AMD Instinct GPU shipments and growth in AMD EPYC CPU sales. The client segment revenue was $1.9 billion, up 29% year-over-year, driven by strong demand for "Zen 5" AMD Ryzen processors ([AMD Reports Third Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1224/amd-reports-third-quarter-2024-financial-results)).

### First Quarter 2024 Financial Results

In the first quarter of 2024, AMD reported revenue of $5.5 billion, with a gross margin of 47% and an operating income of $36 million. The net income for the quarter was $123 million, with diluted earnings per share of $0.07. On a non-GAAP basis, the gross margin was 52%, operating income was $1.1 billion, and net income was $1.0 billion ([AMD Reports First Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-4-30-amd-reports-first-quarter-2024-financial-results.html)).

The data center segment revenue for the first quarter was $2.3 billion, up 80% year-over-year, driven by growth in both AMD Instinct GPUs and 4th Gen AMD EPYC CPUs. The client segment revenue was $1.4 billion, up 85% year-over-year, primarily driven by AMD Ryzen 8000 Series processor sales ([AMD Reports First Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-4-30-amd-reports-first-quarter-2024-financial-results.html)).

## Strategic Initiatives and Acquisitions

### Acquisition of ZT Systems

AMD announced the acquisition of ZT Systems for $4.9 billion, a strategic move aimed at enhancing its data center AI systems capabilities. The acquisition is expected to be accretive to AMD's non-GAAP earnings per share by the end of the fiscal year 2025. ZT Systems, a leading supplier of hyperscale server solutions for cloud computing and artificial intelligence, will join AMD's Data Center Solutions Business Group upon deal completion ([AMD's strategic play: ZT Systems acquisition to expand market reach and strengthen AI business](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/amds-strategic-play-zt-systems-acquisition-expand-market-hurwitz-ekm4e)).

The acquisition is expected to close in the first half of 2025, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. AMD plans to integrate ZT Systems' expertise in full rack deployment for hyperscale data centers, including servers, storage, and switches, to accelerate the deployment of optimized rack-scale solutions ([AMD's strategic play: ZT Systems acquisition to expand market reach and strengthen AI business](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/amds-strategic-play-zt-systems-acquisition-expand-market-hurwitz-ekm4e)).

### AI and Data Center Growth

AMD has been focusing on expanding its AI and data center capabilities. The company announced the availability of the AMD Instinct MI300X accelerators, designed to deliver leadership performance for generative AI workloads and high-performance computing (HPC) applications. Additionally, AMD unveiled the AMD Instinct MI300A APU, which integrates the CPU and GPU cores on a single package, providing an efficient platform for AI training ([AMD Reports Third Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1224/amd-reports-third-quarter-2024-financial-results)).

AMD's strategic initiatives in AI and data center growth are expected to drive significant revenue growth in the coming years. The company's focus on delivering leadership AI solutions across its business is creating substantial growth opportunities as the demand for more compute increases across various markets ([AMD Reports Second Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1209/amd-reports-second-quarter-2024-financial-results)).

## Financial Outlook

### Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2024 Financial Results

AMD is scheduled to report its fiscal fourth quarter and full year 2024 financial results on February 4, 2025. The company expects to deliver record annual revenue growth for 2024, based on significant growth in its Data Center and Client segments. AMD's fourth quarter 2024 financial outlook includes revenue and non-GAAP gross margin expectations ([AMD to Report Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2024 Financial Results](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/amd-report-fiscal-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results-2025-01-14)).

### Future Growth Opportunities

AMD's future growth opportunities are centered around its leadership in high-performance computing, graphics, and AI technologies. The company's strategic acquisitions, such as ZT Systems, and its focus on expanding its AI ecosystem and software capabilities position AMD as a formidable player in the AI and data center markets. The integration of ZT Systems' expertise in hyperscale server solutions will enable AMD to accelerate its deployment of optimized rack-scale solutions, expanding its market reach and enhancing its AI capabilities ([AMD's strategic play: ZT Systems acquisition to expand market reach and strengthen AI business](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/amds-strategic-play-zt-systems-acquisition-expand-market-hurwitz-ekm4e)).

## Conclusion

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) has demonstrated strong financial performance in 2024, driven by its strategic focus on AI and data center growth. The company's acquisitions and product innovations have positioned it for continued success in the high-performance computing and AI markets. With a robust financial outlook and strategic initiatives in place, AMD is well-positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for compute across various industries.
